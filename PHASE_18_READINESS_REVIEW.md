# SpotGo Phase 18: Product and Production Readiness

**Review date:** 2026-11-12
**Scope:** Current workspace snapshot; read-only review and planning.

## Scope and caveats

The working tree is substantially changed: `git diff --stat` showed 80 tracked paths changed, with large extractions from `backend/src/server.js`, `lib/features/app_shell/presentation/news_feed.dart`, `lib/features/merchant_management/presentation/merchant_add_page.dart`, and `lib/features/merchant_management/presentation/merchant_dashboard.dart`, plus deleted files and untracked route/feature modules. Findings below describe this workspace, not necessarily the committed or deployed revision. Reconcile the report with the release commit before acting on it. Only this report and the task registry were written; no product code, configuration, migration, or deployment behavior was changed. No tests were run as part of the audit.

Provider accounts, production configuration, live credentials, device behavior, deployment infrastructure, and external-service delivery cannot be verified from repository source alone. No exhaustive Git-history secret scan was performed, and the ignored local `backend/.env` was not inspected.

## Executive summary

SpotGo has useful customer and merchant breadth, but the journey from social Home to venue booking or match discovery is not immediately obvious. The merchant flow has more concrete booking-state handling than its customer-facing support and notification handoffs.

The clearest release decision is to keep real-money launch blocked: the regular customer payment UI describes a prototype flow, while PayMongo checkout/webhook routes exist in the backend. Before taking real charges, complete and verify the customer checkout, make provider-confirmed events the only payment truth, and close checkout idempotency, refunds, settlement, and reconciliation gaps.

Deadline notifications are best-effort after database commits. Social media upload migration is only partially complete. The documented and implemented backend topology is one API process; process-local limits, caches, and WebSocket delivery do not support safe horizontal scaling without shared coordination.

## Journey maps

### Customer

1. **First use:** guests can browse public community content; following and sharing require signing in (`README.md`). `FirstLaunchOnboardingGate` introduces Sports, Events, and Fitness, then opens the customer shell (`lib/features/app_shell/presentation/app_root.dart`, `lib/features/app_shell/presentation/first_launch_onboarding.dart`). Home is a social experience feed with Latest/Following; the separate Venue destination opens venue discovery (`lib/features/app_shell/presentation/customer_navigation_shell.dart`, `lib/features/app_shell/presentation/customer_home_page.dart`). Returning customers with a saved booking type can land on the venue feed. Find Match is also a Home filter, not a peer navigation destination (`customer_home_page.dart`, `lib/features/app_shell/presentation/news_feed.dart`). The destinations exist, but the distinction between social content, venue inventory, and match discovery needs stronger first-use cues.
2. **Booking:** guests may browse public venue cards, but signing in is required before selecting a booking date/time (`test/news_feed_guest_browse_test.dart`). The booking path continues through date/time selection, request creation, merchant decision, payment obligation, and the customer Bookings page (`lib/features/bookings/presentation/`, `lib/features/app_shell/presentation/news_feed_venue_cards.dart`, `lib/features/bookings/presentation/customer_bookings_page.dart`). The key lifecycle is request/pending → approved or declined/expired → applicable online payment or cash-on-arrival → arrival, no-show, or completion; reviews are gated on completion. Pending, Approved, and History lists expose loading/error recovery and payment actions.
3. **Matchmaking:** users can find or host sports matches and filter by sport, date, and location. A join request is distinct from a confirmed place; the lobby list has empty/error recovery (`lib/features/bookings/presentation/matchmaking_lobby_list_page.dart`). Merchant approval precedes the host downpayment flow, which has a 15-minute payment window (`lib/features/merchant_management/presentation/merchant_dashboard_matchmaking.dart`).
4. **After booking:** customers check in with a venue QR code, then can review completed bookings (`lib/features/profile/presentation/profile_dashboard.dart`, `lib/features/social/presentation/reviews.dart`, `lib/features/bookings/presentation/customer_bookings_page.dart`). Booking reminders, match alerts, and social notifications are reached through separate UI entry points.

### Merchant

1. **Set up:** merchant account and business details lead into venue management and News Card publication; the dashboard action center links to booking requests, payments, and unfinished News Cards (`lib/features/merchant_management/presentation/merchant_dashboard_analytics_section.dart`, `lib/features/merchant_management/presentation/merchant_add_page.dart`).
2. **Decide:** Manage separates Booking, Customer, and Payment work. Booking requests have approval deadlines, review/action controls, and payment eligibility constraints (`lib/features/merchant_management/presentation/merchant_dashboard.dart`, `merchant_dashboard_booking_management.dart`).
3. **Operate and finish:** merchants review a pending request, approve or decline it within its deadline and payment constraints, then track customer payment/arrival and the final no-show or completion state. Arrivals depend on customer QR check-in; reviews are gated on completed bookings (`lib/features/merchant_management/presentation/merchant_dashboard_booking_management.dart`, `lib/features/social/presentation/reviews.dart`).
4. **Recover:** booking screens expose retry/refresh in several failure states, but the merchant Help Center is a placeholder and refund guidance tells customers to contact support without providing a working route (`merchant_dashboard.dart`, `lib/features/bookings/presentation/customer_bookings_page.dart`).

## Prioritized follow-up backlog

Each item is a focused implementation task, not authorization to change provider defaults or deploy. Acceptance criteria are intended to be testable in the relevant workstream.

### P0 — before real-money payments

1. **Connect customer checkout to provider-confirmed payment state.**
   **Impact:** prevents demo UI or client assertions from being mistaken for real charges. **Evidence:** `prototype_payment_dialog.dart`, `customer_bookings_page.dart`, and `backend/src/routes/payment_routes.js`; matchmaking downpayment already has a separate external-checkout path (`lib/features/merchant_management/presentation/merchant_dashboard_matchmaking.dart`). **Scope:** complete the missing regular booking flow and verify both customer payment paths use the appropriate provider endpoint; make signed provider events authoritative; keep prototype payment unavailable in production. **Dependencies:** provider sandbox credentials, webhook registration, booking/payment state contract. **Acceptance:** sandbox tests prove a successful signed event updates only the matching booking/amount/currency; client-side success alone cannot mark payment paid; production rejects prototype payment.

2. **Make checkout, refund, and settlement recoverable and reconcilable.**
   **Impact:** prevents duplicate/orphan charges and unreconciled customer or merchant balances. **Evidence:** checkout session persistence in `payment_routes.js`; cancellation/refund paths in `booking_routes.js` and `server.js`; payment fields in `database/booking.sql`. **Scope:** provider idempotency/session reuse, durable refund status recovery, a transaction ledger and merchant settlement/reconciliation definition. **Dependencies:** P0-1 provider-confirmed checkout and agreed settlement/refund policy. **Acceptance:** retry/race tests prove one charge per booking intent; periodic reconciliation identifies provider/database mismatches; refunds reach a verifiable terminal state; settlement totals reconcile to provider records before launch.

### P1 — before broader launch

3. **Add a durable notification outbox for critical booking events.**
   **Impact:** prevents approval/payment deadline messages being lost between database commit and provider dispatch. **Evidence:** `booking_approval_deadlines.js`, `booking_payment_deadlines.js`, `booking_notification_service.js`, and `backend/README.md`. **Scope:** write notification intents transactionally, retry with bounded backoff, record terminal failures, and use provider idempotency where available. **Dependencies:** schema migration and provider retry policy. **Acceptance:** tests simulate process failure after commit and provider timeouts; events are retried without duplicate customer messages and are observable to operators.

4. **Make venue booking and match discovery first-class Home actions.**
   **Impact:** guests may confuse the social Home with marketplace inventory or miss match discovery. **Evidence:** `customer_navigation_shell.dart`, `customer_home_page.dart`, `first_launch_onboarding.dart`, `news_feed_venue_cards.dart`; current navigation coverage in `test/news_feed_navigation_test.dart`. **Scope:** preserve separate social, Venue, and Find Match destinations while making their purposes discoverable in first-use and persistent navigation. **Dependencies:** product decision on labels and whether Find Match becomes a peer destination or remains a clearly signposted Home action. **Acceptance:** a fresh guest reaches venues and matches from Home in at most two actions; tests confirm social posts are not presented as venue inventory and guest booking still prompts for sign-in at the booking boundary.

5. **Provide a real support and notification handoff.**
   **Impact:** users cannot resolve booking/refund failures from placeholder Help Center and “contact support” copy; booking and social notifications are split across separate destinations. **Evidence:** `merchant_dashboard.dart`, `customer_bookings_page.dart`, `customer_home_page.dart`, and `profile_dashboard.dart`. **Scope:** add an actionable support route that carries a booking reference; label social, booking, and match notifications distinctly and route each to the relevant item. **Dependencies:** support channel ownership, privacy policy for booking details in support, and agreed notification taxonomy. **Acceptance:** customer refund and merchant help actions open a working support path; approval, payment, and match notifications open the corresponding booking/match in tests.

6. **Complete social-media migration with a compatibility and rollback gate.**
   **Impact:** base64 legacy uploads can prolong storage cost/compatibility risk and make rollout state unclear. **Evidence:** `social_media_storage.js`, `.env.example`, `migrate-social-media.js`, and `backend/README.md`. **Scope:** inventory legacy records, migrate and verify reads, expose migration progress/failures, document rollback, and disable legacy writes only after supported clients are verified. **Dependencies:** bucket IAM/CORS/lifecycle setup, backup, and app-version compatibility window. **Acceptance:** migration is repeatable and resumable; image hashes/visibility are verified; legacy-write disablement and rollback are tested in staging.

7. **Establish production operations and provider configuration gates.**
   **Impact:** source-level configuration cannot guarantee a safe deployment or recovery. **Evidence:** `backend/README.md`, `.github/workflows/dependency-scan.yml`, `README.md`, `backend/.env.example`, and `android/app/google-services.json`; runtime controls and requirements include `JWT_SECRET`, `CLIENT_ORIGIN`, `TENOR_API_KEY`, Firebase credentials, SMTP/Twilio credentials, and PayMongo keys. **Scope:** deploy checklist/runbooks for MySQL migration and privileges, secret provisioning/rotation, HTTPS and exact CORS origins, gateway/IP rate limiting, Firebase project/API-key restrictions and FCM, email/SMS, Tenor, PayMongo webhook registration, monitoring, alerts, backups, restore, and incident response. **Dependencies:** target hosting environment and provider accounts. **Acceptance:** staging deployment records successful migrations, health/smoke checks, alert delivery, backup restoration, webhook/provider configuration, and secret access without committing credentials; Firebase client configuration is confirmed for the intended project and appropriately restricted.

8. **Make permanently denied location permission recoverable.**
   **Impact:** users can lose location-assisted discovery without a clear path back while venue browsing should remain usable. **Evidence:** `all_venues_page.dart`, `matchmaking_lobby_list_page.dart`, and `test/responsive_layout_test.dart`. **Scope:** distinguish denied, permanently denied, and location-services-off; preserve manual browsing and provide app-settings actions when needed. **Dependencies:** platform-specific permission APIs and copy. **Acceptance:** widget/device coverage verifies all three states, manual browsing remains available, and permanently denied users can open system settings.

### P2 — before horizontal scaling

9. **Coordinate process-local services before adding API instances.**
   **Impact:** independent instances would diverge in rate limits, caches, WebSocket events, and scheduled deadline work. **Evidence:** `backend/README.md`, `backend/src/server.js`, and `backend/src/booking_approval_deadlines.js`. **Scope:** shared rate limiting/cache invalidation, a broker-backed live-update bus, and single-owner or leased deadline processing; preserve conditional database state transitions. **Dependencies:** deployment topology and load/concurrency targets. **Acceptance:** multi-instance integration tests prove cross-instance invalidation/event delivery, one deadline transition/notification per booking, and consistent throttling before a second production instance is enabled.

10. **Set database and service capacity from measured load.**
    **Impact:** adding instances without understanding DB pool pressure and query contention can worsen outages. **Evidence:** MySQL queries/migrations in `backend/src/` and `backend/src/schema_migrations.js`; current CI uses MySQL integration but no load evidence is documented. **Scope:** profile high-volume booking/payment/news queries, tune indexes/pool limits from measurements, and define saturation limits. **Dependencies:** representative workload and single-instance baseline. **Acceptance:** repeatable load tests meet agreed latency/error/connection thresholds and document database saturation and recovery behavior.

### Ongoing quality improvements

11. **Extract large screens/routes incrementally around tested boundaries.**
    **Impact:** large modules make changes risky, but broad rewrites threaten booking/payment behavior. **Evidence:** current diff shows large reductions in `server.js`, `news_feed.dart`, `merchant_add_page.dart`, and `merchant_dashboard.dart`, plus untracked extracted modules. **Scope:** first reconcile and land intended current extractions; then split by stable route, controller, or presentation boundary with dependency injection and focused regression tests. **Dependencies:** confirm the intended untracked modules are included in the release commit. **Acceptance:** each extraction preserves public behavior and its focused tests; no new cross-feature `part` coupling; module ownership and route registration are explicit.

12. **Extend journey-wide accessibility, localization, and device validation.**
    **Impact:** representative responsive tests do not guarantee every booking, arrival, error, and permission screen works with assistive technology or large text. **Evidence:** `test/responsive_layout_test.dart`, `test/app_localization_test.dart`, `test/customer_navigation_shell_test.dart`, `test/matchmaking_lobby_test.dart`, and representative `AppText` use in Flutter screens. **Scope:** expand screen-reader labels, localized status/error text, phone-width/large-text cases, and real-device permission/notification behavior across customer and merchant journeys. **Dependencies:** prioritized screen inventory and target locale/device matrix. **Acceptance:** identified critical flows have passing widget/accessibility checks at narrow widths and large text; device checks cover notification/permission differences and are reported separately from CI.

## Provider and deployment checks requiring external validation

Before any real-money release, verify PayMongo sandbox and live-mode behavior, webhook registration/signatures, refund completion, settlement terms, and reconciliation against provider records. Verify HTTPS/TLS termination, exact deployed CORS origins, provider-mode secret protection and rotation, gateway enforcement and forwarded-IP handling. Confirm `android/app/google-services.json` points to the intended Firebase project and that client API keys have appropriate restrictions. Verify Firebase Storage bucket IAM, CORS, lifecycle policies, Firebase/FCM/APNs setup, Twilio sender eligibility, Gmail sender-account readiness, and Tenor key provisioning. Separately verify production MySQL network exposure, least-privilege accounts, migration permissions, backups/restores, monitoring, alert routing, incident response, and device-level navigation, accessibility, and location permission behavior.
